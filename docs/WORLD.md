# Connected-world direction

Implemented: Gatewater's civic district; Observatory → Rootfoundry workshop → Cooled Ledger → Archive, with the last reciprocal route opened from inside. The workshop's ability/descent/lift circuit and the Ledger's physical return make backtracking part of the world.

Planned: further Rootfoundry routes and a regional boss; Salt Cathedral, Mirror Fen, Black Meridian, Ash Gardens and the convergent Deep Bell. These names are a development direction, not playable-content claims. New abilities should make earlier routes useful again, and each region should eventually connect to more than one neighboring region. Preserve stable room IDs and migrate old saves conservatively.

Original creative identity: a copper lantern custodian crosses a drowned bell-city. Names survive in copper, living roots and civic records. Amber foreground cues and cooler distant machinery distinguish walkable architecture from scenery. Reference works informed goals for readable silhouettes, responsive movement, connected exploration and environmental story; no game assets, characters, music or maps were extracted.
